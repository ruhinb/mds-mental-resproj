
Project Objectives

1. Construct and preprocess datasets suitable for analysis, including:
	•	the Beyond Blue mental-health forum dataset of posts and comments, and
	•	a curated dataset of Australian natural disaster events with harmonised time windows and standardised disaster-type labels.

2. Apply transformer-based emotion models to extract emotional signals and project them into the Circumplex Model of Affect for interpretable affective analysis.

3. Examine temporal changes in users’ emotional states across post sequences, identifying patterns of improvement, deterioration, or stability in community interactions.

4. Investigate how natural disaster contexts influence emotional expression, using comparative analyses across disaster phases and interrupted-time-series modelling.
